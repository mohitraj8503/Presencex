Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 21, 2026

PresenceX ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Collection

We use Google OAuth to access your Google Sheets. We do not store your Google credentials on our servers. All tokens are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB/LocalStorage.

2. Use of Data

We only access the specific spreadsheets you link to PresenceX for the purpose of recording attendance.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Identity Services for authentication. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.
