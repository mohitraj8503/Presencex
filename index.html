🚀 Quick Start Guide

1. Select Subject: Choose your subject from the dropdown menu at the top.

2. Mark Attendance: Click on student names to toggle status:

  • First click → Present (Green)
  • Second click → Absent (Red)
  • Third click → Unmarked (Gray)

3. Quick Actions: Use "Mark All Present" or "Mark All Absent" buttons for bulk updates.

4. Generate Report: Click "Generate Report" to see a summary and copyable text list.

5. Export: Use "Export to Excel" to save the attendance record.

💡 Pro Tip: Use the Filter buttons to view specific groups (e.g., see only "Absent" students).

📥 Excel Import Guide

Step 1: Prepare Your File

Ensure your Excel file has columns for Roll Number and Student Name.

Step 2: Import & Configure

Click "Import Excel", select your file, and choose the correct sheet. Map students using Roll Number (recommended) or Name.

Step 3: Analyze & Fill

The system will detect headers and match students. Review the analysis and click "Confirm & Fill".

🔌 Troubleshooting:
• "Roll not found"? Check if your Excel has a distinct header row.
• "Duplicate rolls"? Clean your Excel to have unique roll numbers.
• "Date column not found"? A new column will be created automatically.

📤 Exporting

Click "Export to Excel" to download the current attendance sheet. Statuses are clearly marked (Present/Absent/Unmarked).

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does my data get saved online?
A: No! Everything is 100% local in your browser. No data is sent to any server.

Q: Can I use this offline?
A: Yes! After the first load, the app works completely offline.

Q: What if I accidentally close the tab?
A: Auto-save keeps your work safe. You'll get a resume prompt when you open it again.

Q: Can I edit the student list?
A: Not directly in the app, but you can import your own Excel with any student list you want.

Q: How do I share attendance?
A: Generate a report and copy the text, or export the Excel file to send via email/WhatsApp.
